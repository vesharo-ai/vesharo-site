## Content Integrity & Technical Standards
1. **Authorship**: Exclusively written by technical leadership (Harsh Siddhapura or Hitesh Chauhan).
2. **Schema & Syndication**: Valid Schema.org `Article` JSON-LD embedded on every route with RFC-822 timestamps in `rss.xml`.
3. **Internal Linking**: Every article links back to relevant service pages (`/services/mobile-app-development`, `/services/ai-automation`) and real verified case studies (`/portfolio/lockapp`, `/portfolio/navratrios`, `/portfolio/wirebench`).
4. **No Thin Content**: Every article includes executable code snippets (TypeScript, Python, Bash), concrete architectural trade-offs, and actionable checklists.
